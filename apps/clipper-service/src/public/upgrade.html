Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods are supported? ▾
Clipper Studio uses Stripe as the payment processor, supporting global Visa/Mastercard/Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Link.
How do I get my license key after paying? ▾
Your subscription unlocks automatically via Stripe Checkout or webhook! You can also paste your Stripe Checkout Session ID (cs_...) or Subscription ID (sub_...) above.
Can I upgrade from Pro to Ultra? ▾
Yes! Click the "Upgrade to Ultra" button above. After payment, you'll receive a new Ultra license key. Activate it on this page — your Pro license will be automatically replaced.
Is there a refund policy? ▾
Yes — 7-day money-back guarantee. If Clipper Studio doesn't work for your setup, contact us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund via Stripe.
Can I use one license on multiple machines? ▾
Each license key is for one installation. Pro licenses allow 2 activations (e.g. laptop + desktop), Ultra allows 3 activations. Contact support to transfer a license.
